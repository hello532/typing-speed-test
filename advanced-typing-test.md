# Advanced Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/advanced-typing-test.html

For people who already type well: a demanding passage and honest scoring to see whether you belong in the top percentile.

## Built for experienced typists

If you already clear 60 WPM comfortably, an easy test tells you nothing. This advanced test uses longer, richer passages and expects full command of capitals, punctuation and the number row — a genuine stretch for a practised touch typist.

## Targets for advanced typists

Professional transcriptionists, programmers and typists working from dictation live between 80 and 120 WPM. An advanced target is 80 WPM or more at 98% accuracy; anything above 100 WPM is elite and rare.

## Where advanced speed is really won

At this level you are not limited by finger speed but by attention: reading far ahead, holding rhythm through difficult phrases, and never looking at your hands. Small reductions in hesitation, not faster fingers, are what move an advanced score upward.

## Use it as a true benchmark

Take this test monthly and record your net WPM. Because the passages are demanding and consistent, a rising score here is a far better signal of genuine progress than a lucky 10-second sprint.

## Advanced Typing Test FAQ

### What is an advanced typing speed?

Around 80 words per minute with 98% accuracy or better is advanced. Above 100 WPM is elite; professional transcriptionists and programmers often sit in the 80 to 120 WPM range.

### How is this different from an easy test?

The passages are longer and richer in capitals, punctuation and vocabulary, with number-row reaches, so it demands real technique rather than fast fingers alone.

### How often should I test?

Once a month is enough. Advanced skill changes slowly, and a monthly net-WPM reading gives a stable, meaningful benchmark.
