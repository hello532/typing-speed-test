# 70 WPM Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/70-wpm-typing-test.html

Seventy words per minute is the 82nd percentile — faster than four out of five measured typists, and the entry band for general transcription. At this level the limiting factor stops being your fingers and becomes your rhythm.

Seventy words per minute is 350 characters per minute, 21,000 keystrokes per hour, and roughly the 82nd percentile of the CHI 2018 sample. It is faster than the top-quintile boundary and still 8 WPM short of the top decile at 78.

That last stretch is the hardest in typing: the 70-to-80 range typically takes months rather than weeks, because the easy inefficiencies are already gone.

Below 60 WPM most gains come from removing bad habits — looking down, backspacing, two-finger reaches. Above 70 there are no bad habits left to remove; the work is consistency. A 70 WPM typist's speed varies by 15-20% between rounds, and the variance itself is what holds the average down.

So the metric to watch changes from peak WPM to the spread between your best and worst round. Narrowing that spread raises the average faster than trying to sprint.

Ten consecutive one-minute rounds, once a week. Record all ten. The number that matters is the lowest, not the highest.
Hard-text rounds: quotes with punctuation, code, or pangrams. Speed on easy sentences is not transferable; speed on awkward text is.
Audio transcription practice: transcribe two minutes of speech and time it. Spoken English runs at 130-160 WPM, so 70 WPM typing leaves you well behind real-time — which is why transcriptionists use foot pedals and text expanders.
Text expansion: learn five short forms for phrases you type constantly. At this level, tooling beats training.

Transcription is advertised at 65-80 WPM, but the measurement is over audio with playback control, not over visible text. The skill gap is short-term memory: you hear a phrase, hold it, and type it, which is harder than copying.

If transcription is the goal, test yourself on audio rather than on this page. If it is not, 70 WPM already clears every clerical requirement and further speed is a hobby.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 70 WPM fast?

Yes — about the 82nd percentile, faster than four in five measured typists. It is the entry band for general transcription and well above every clerical requirement.

### How many keystrokes per hour is 70 WPM?

21,000 KPH. Seventy words per minute is 350 characters per minute, and 350 × 60 = 21,000.

### How long does it take to get from 70 to 80 WPM?

Usually months, not weeks. The easy inefficiencies are gone by 70; remaining gains come from consistency, hard-text practice and tooling such as text expansion.

### Is 70 WPM enough for transcription?

It is the entry band for general transcription, which advertises 65-80 WPM. Medical and legal transcription often expect the upper end plus domain vocabulary.

### Why does my score swing 10 WPM between rounds?

Variance is normal and grows with speed. Track your lowest of ten rounds rather than your best; narrowing the spread is what raises a 70 WPM average toward 80.
