## Quotes typing test

A quotes typing test measures your speed and accuracy on real famous quotations — the same sentences people actually read, share and write. Unlike random word drills, quotes make you type the way real writing flows: natural word order, commas and full stops in the right places, and capital letters that mark where thoughts begin.

## Why quotes are the best typing practice text

Quotes are dense with the two things that slow most typists down: punctuation and capitals. Every sentence forces you to handle them at speed, which is exactly the skill that separates a fast prose typist from a fast word-list typist. And because the lines are memorable, your brain reads ahead while your fingers follow — the same look-ahead rhythm that professional typists rely on.

The pool on this page mixes short, punchy lines with longer reflective ones, so a one-minute round trains both burst speed and sustained rhythm. If you want a warm-up first, the quick brown fox typing test covers every letter of the alphabet in a single sentence.

## What is in this quote collection

Thirty-five lines from public-domain and widely attributed authors — Twain, Aristotle, Franklin, Lincoln, Edison, Einstein, Lao Tzu, Marcus Aurelius and more. Each one is typed exactly as commonly cited, with the author's name after a dash, so you also practise the dash-and-capital pattern that citations use.

## How to use quotes to raise your WPM

Take one minute a day. Type the quote as if you were writing it down for someone else to read: clean capitals, clean punctuation, no shortcuts. Watch the weak-key report after each round — most people discover a specific symbol (the comma, the dash, the shift key) that taxes every sentence they type. Drill that, and your prose speed jumps within a week.
