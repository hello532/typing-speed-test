# Quotes Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/quotes-typing-test.html

Test your typing speed with famous quotes from history's greatest minds. Real sentences with natural punctuation and capitals. Free, instant results, no sign up.

A word-list test removes everything except letter-finding: no capitals, no punctuation, no sentence structure. A quotes test keeps all three, which is what a document actually contains.

The difference shows up in the score. Most typists read 5-10% lower on quotes than on plain sentences at the same effort, because every capital needs Shift with the opposite hand and every comma pulls the right little finger off the home row. That gap is not a weakness in the typist; it is the cost of real text, and it is the number that transfers to work.

Thirty-five public-domain quotations, between roughly 40 and 140 characters each, chosen so the punctuation is natural rather than decorative. They include attributions (a dash and a name), which is exactly the kind of trailing detail that gets dropped under time pressure.

The quotes are short by design. A quote round is usually 60-120 seconds, so a passage you can finish is better than a paragraph you abandon - and finishing gives you an accuracy figure over a complete unit of text.

WPM counts five characters as a word, including the space. That convention does not care whether the characters are letters or punctuation, so a comma costs exactly as much as a letter - which is why quote scores feel harsh.

Errors are counted per character: a missing apostrophe, a straight quote typed where a curly one appears, or a capital typed as lowercase all count. If you want a score that reflects real writing, that is the right behaviour.

Exam passages and workplace documents include capitals, punctuation and names. Practising on quotes builds the three habits those passages punish: Shift discipline, the comma reach, and reading ahead enough to know a capital is coming.

Run one quote round and one plain-sentence round back to back. If the gap is more than 8%, punctuation is your weak link and the punctuation test is the drill. If the gap is small and both scores are low, the problem is speed, not punctuation.

No song lyrics, no film dialogue, no modern quotations. Everything in the pool is public domain, so there is nothing here that cannot be reproduced, and nothing that will quietly disappear from the site when someone asserts a copyright claim.

## Quotes typing test FAQ

### What is a quotes typing test?

A test using real quotations as the text, so capitals, commas, semicolons and quotation marks are part of the exercise - which plain word lists remove.

### Why is my quote score lower than my normal score?

Punctuation and capitals add keystrokes that a word list does not have. A 5-10% drop is normal; more than that means punctuation is a specific weakness worth drilling.

### Are the quotes copyrighted?

No. Every quotation in the pool is public domain, and each includes its attribution where one is traditionally given.

### How long should a quotes round be?

Sixty to 120 seconds. The quotes are short enough to finish, which gives you an accuracy figure over a complete piece of text rather than a fragment.

### Does punctuation count as an error?

Yes - an omitted or wrong capital, comma, apostrophe or quotation mark counts as an error, exactly as it would in a document or an exam passage.
