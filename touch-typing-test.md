## touch typing test

A touch typing test measures how fast and how accurately you type without looking at the keys. Touch typing means all ten fingers work from the home row, and your eyes stay on the screen the whole time. This free test runs for 60 seconds, scores your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy live, and highlights every error so you can see exactly which keys still trip you up. Because the practice sentences are taken from real touch typing lessons, taking the test is itself a lesson.

## Why take a touch typing test every day

Muscle memory is built by frequency, not by marathon sessions. A daily 60 second touch typing test is enough to keep the home row fresh and to expose the two or three keys that always slow you down. Track your WPM across a week and you will see the pattern: accuracy climbs first, and speed follows a few days behind. When your accuracy passes 98 percent, you know the keyboard has finally moved into your fingers.

## How to use this touch typing practice

Start by resting your index fingers on the small bumps on F and J, with the other fingers curling onto the home row beside them. Type the sentence on screen without glancing down; if you must look, look only after the line is finished. Keep your wrists floating and your shoulders down. After the test, read the error report: those keys, not your fastest ones, are your next practice targets.

## Touch typing test FAQ

### What is a touch typing test?

A touch typing test measures your typing speed and accuracy while you type without looking at the keyboard. This one runs for 60 seconds and reports live WPM, accuracy, and a per-key error summary.

### Is this touch typing test free?

Yes, it is completely free. There is no sign up, no account, and no paywall, and your results are stored only in your own browser.

### How fast is good touch typing?

A comfortable touch typing speed is 40 to 60 WPM, and 70 to 90 WPM is considered fast. Above 100 WPM is competitive territory. Accuracy matters first: most people reach their best speed only after their accuracy passes 97 to 98 percent.

### How long until I can touch type at full speed?

Expect the home row within a week and 40 to 60 WPM in three to four weeks of 10 to 15 minute daily sessions. Full speed takes longer, but this test doubles as training since every round uses real lesson sentences.

### Should I look at the keyboard while testing?

No. Cover the keyboard with a piece of paper if you have to, and let the bumps on F and J guide your index fingers. Every glance down is a habit that costs speed later, so train the eyes to stay on the screen.

### What is the home row?

The home row is the middle letter row: A S D F G H J K L ; with the thumbs on the space bar. Every finger owns a small group of keys around the home row, and every finger returns home after each keystroke.
