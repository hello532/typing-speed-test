# Hard Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/hard-typing-test.html

Easy text hides weak fingers. This hard test adds capitals, symbols and numbers so every part of the keyboard gets a workout.

## Why the text is harder

Real documents are not lists of clean short words. They contain capital letters, commas, apostrophes, dashes, numbers and long multi-syllable words. This hard test mixes all of them so you practise the reaches that a simple word test never touches.

## What a hard test exposes

A sudden shift to a capital sends your hand away from the home row; a number row stretch breaks your rhythm; heavy punctuation makes you hunt for a key you rarely use. Those are exactly the moments where speed and accuracy leak away in real work.

## How to score well on hard text

Slow your overall rate just enough to keep accuracy high, and use the correct fingers even for the awkward keys — the number row and both little fingers are the usual weak spots. Practising hard text on purpose is how easy text becomes effortless.

## When to step up to hard mode

If you already hold 90%+ accuracy on normal passages and want to keep improving, hard mixed text is your next challenge. Expect your WPM to dip at first; that is the point — you are training new reaches, and speed returns higher.

## Hard Typing Test FAQ

### What makes a typing test hard?

Difficult tests include capital letters, numbers, symbols, uncommon words and punctuation, forcing reaches far from the home row instead of simple repeated words.

### Why is my WPM lower on hard text?

Because your hand has to leave the home row for capitals and the number row, and unusual words slow your reading ahead. A lower score on hard text is normal.

### Should I practise hard typing tests?

Yes, if you already type normal text accurately. Hard passages train the reaches that ordinary practice ignores, which raises your ceiling.
