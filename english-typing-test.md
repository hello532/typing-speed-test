# English Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/english-typing-test.html

Test your English typing speed with real sentences. Measure your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy instantly. Free, no sign up.

For most of the world, an English typing test is simply a typing test. But in South Asia - and especially in India - "English typing test" is a specific exam format: 35 words per minute in English (or 30 in Hindi) for clerical posts, taken on a standard keyboard for ten minutes, with a small error allowance.

That is why this page treats English as one option among several rather than as the default. If you are preparing for an exam, the exam's rules matter more than the language label.

Requirement | Speed | Length | Notes
SSC CHSL LDC / JSA (English) | 35 WPM | 10 minutes | Qualifying, error allowance applies
SSC CHSL (Hindi) | 30 WPM | 10 minutes | Same test, lower threshold
SSC DEO | 8,000 key depressions/hour | 15 minutes | Corrections count as depressions
US federal clerical | 40 WPM | 5 minutes | No more than three errors
General office | 40-55 WPM | 3-5 minutes | Net WPM, 95% accuracy

Notice that the same English text produces three different measurements: words per minute, key depressions per hour, and a five-minute sample with a hard error cap. Convert before comparing - 8,000 KPDH is about 27 WPM.

English on QWERTY is the best-supported layout in the world, which is why most speed records are set on it. The characters that decide accuracy are not the letters but the punctuation: the apostrophe, the comma, the full stop and the capital that follows it.

A useful drill is sentence case with quotes. Most candidates lose 3-5 WPM there, and it is the exact content of an exam passage. Practise it weekly rather than daily - it is a check, not a workout.

Use the same length as the exam (ten minutes for SSC, five for federal), type a passage with full punctuation, and score net WPM plus errors. Then repeat on a different keyboard at least once: exam centres do not provide your laptop.

If your error count is the problem rather than your speed, stop chasing WPM for two weeks and run accuracy rounds at 85% of your pace. A clean 37 WPM passes; a 45 WPM run with 12% errors does not.

## Typing test FAQ

### What is the typing speed required for English typing tests in exams?

35 words per minute for SSC CHSL LDC and JSA posts, over ten minutes, with a small error allowance. US federal clerical roles ask 40 WPM over five minutes with at most three errors.

### Is English typing speed measured in WPM or KPH?

Both, depending on the post. SSC DEO uses 8,000 key depressions per hour; clerical and federal roles use words per minute. 8,000 KPDH is about 27 WPM.

### How many mistakes are allowed?

A small allowance, commonly around 5% of the words typed, and it is stated in the notice for that exam cycle. The federal standard is stricter: three errors in five minutes.

### Does punctuation count in an English typing test?

Yes. Capitals and punctuation are part of the passage and an omitted character is an error. Practise sentence case with quotes, which is where most candidates lose their margin.

### Can I use my own keyboard in the exam?

No. Centre equipment is used. Rehearse on a different keyboard at least twice so key travel and layout are not a surprise.
