# Pangram Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/pangram-typing-test.html

Practice typing with pangrams — sentences that contain every letter of the alphabet. From the famous quick brown fox to fifteen harder variations. Free, instant results.

A pangram contains every letter of the alphabet at least once. That makes it the shortest text that touches every key you own, which is exactly what a warm-up should do: no letter gets skipped because the passage happened not to contain it.

It also exposes weak keys honestly. On ordinary prose you can drift for a minute without pressing Q, X or Z; on a pangram you press all of them, and the error report has something to say.

Pangram | Why use it
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog | The classic - short, all 26 letters, quick to finish
Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow | Shorter, punctuation included, good for a 15-second round
Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs | Letter-dense with unusual letters in the home-row reach
How vexingly quick daft zebras jump | Modern, all 26 letters, tests the Z and X reaches
Waltz, bad nymph, for quick jigs vex | Short and punctuation-heavy

Use two or three rather than all sixteen. Repetition of the same pangram is what builds the reflex; variety only prevents boredom.

A pangram round is short, so it reads high - typically 5-10 WPM above your five-minute score. Use it as a warm-up and a key check, never as the number you quote.

Best routine: one pangram at maximum speed to wake up, one at 80% pace with zero errors, then normal text for the actual measurement.

The quick brown fox dates from the late nineteenth century and was used to test typewriters and telegraphs; the shorter ones are modern inventions written to be typed. All of them are public domain and safe to reproduce.

## Pangram typing test FAQ

### What is a pangram typing test?

A test using sentences that contain every letter of the alphabet, so one short round touches every key and exposes weak ones.

### What is the shortest pangram?

The shortest commonly used ones are around 30 letters, such as "Waltz, bad nymph, for quick jigs vex". The classic quick brown fox is 35 letters plus spaces.

### Why is my pangram score higher than my normal score?

Short rounds avoid fatigue and drift, and a memorised sentence is easier to type than unfamiliar prose. Expect 5-10 WPM above a five-minute score.

### How many pangrams should I practise?

Two or three. Repeating the same one builds the reflex; cycling through many mainly prevents boredom.
