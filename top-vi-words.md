## Why drill the top Vietnamese words

In almost every language, a few hundred words cover most of what people actually write. Vietnamese is no exception: these 200 words appear again and again in messages, documents, subtitles and web pages. If your fingers can produce them without thinking, your real-world Vietnamese typing speed rises immediately.

Vietnamese high-frequency words are short syllables packed with tones and diacritics (tôi, không, được, rồi). Drilling them trains the tone-mark automaticity that real Vietnamese typing speed is made of.
