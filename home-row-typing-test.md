# Home Row Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/home-row-typing-test.html

The home row is the row of keys your fingers rest on &mdash; A S D F for the left hand and J K L ; for the right. Every key on the keyboard is reached from here, which is why home-row practice is the first lesson in touch typing. Take the timed test below to drill the home row and track your progress.

## What is the home row?

The home row (also called the home keys) is the middle row of the keyboard where your fingers rest between keystrokes: A S D F for the left hand and J K L ; for the right hand. The F and J keys have small raised bumps so your index fingers can find home by feel alone, without looking down.

Home-row typing is the foundation of touch typing. Each finger owns a column of keys and always returns to its home key, so the hand never has to hunt for a letter. Start by typing only home-row letters until they feel automatic, then add the rows above and below.

## Which finger types which home key?

Home key | Finger | Also reaches
A | Left pinky | Q, Z, 1
S | Left ring | W, X, 2
D | Left middle | E, C, 3
F | Left index | R, T, G, V, B, 4, 5
J | Right index | Y, U, H, N, M, 6, 7
K | Right middle | I, comma, 8
L | Right ring | O, full stop, 9
; | Right pinky | P, slash, 0

The thumbs rest on the space bar. Keeping this assignment is what lets you type without looking - see the full method on how to type without looking.

## Home row drills and practice

Effective home-row practice repeats the eight home keys in short, varied bursts rather than long paragraphs, so your fingers build the exact movements they need. Run this test for 1 or 2 minutes a day, aiming first for perfect accuracy and only then for speed. When the home row feels natural, move on to the touch typing test and the keyboard typing test, which introduce the surrounding rows with an on-screen finger guide.

## Home row FAQ

### What are the home row keys?

A, S, D, F for the left hand and J, K, L, ; for the right hand, with the thumbs on the space bar. F and J have raised bumps so you can find the row without looking.

### Why is the home row important?

It is the base position of touch typing. Every key is reached from home and the fingers return there, so you never have to look down to find a letter.

### Which fingers type the home row?

Left hand: pinky on A, ring on S, middle on D, index on F. Right hand: index on J, middle on K, ring on L, pinky on ;.

### How long does it take to learn the home row?

Most people feel comfortable within a few days of short daily practice, but full touch typing across all rows takes a few weeks of consistent practice.

### Should I look at the keyboard when practising?

No. Keep your eyes on the screen and use the F and J bumps to find home. Looking down slows the muscle memory you are trying to build.
