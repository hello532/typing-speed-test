# Typing Test for Paralegals

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/typing-test-for-paralegal.html

Paralegal typing is document work: exhibit lists, citations, numbered paragraphs and defined terms. Firms look for 50–65 WPM, but the errors that matter are formatting errors, not slow words.

## The requirement, and what sits behind it

Paralegal and legal assistant adverts commonly quote 50-65 WPM, below the 60-75 band for legal secretaries because the role includes more research and drafting than pure transcription. Accuracy expectations are high in both, because a document that misnames a party is a real problem.

That is why the useful practice is not speed drills on random sentences but repeated typing of the structures you will actually produce: a caption block, a numbered list with consistent indentation, a citation with the right punctuation, a table of exhibits.

## Formatting is the skill being tested

Numbering. Auto-numbering that renumbers when you paste is the most common way a filing goes out wrong.
Defined terms. "the Company" keeps its capital; the rest of the document depends on it.
Citations. Italics, full stops and abbreviations follow a house style, and consistency is the whole point.
Track changes. Typing inside a tracked document is slower and less forgiving than typing a blank page.

## A rehearsal that matches the work

Take one real clause with three sub-paragraphs and a defined term, and type it three times: once for accuracy, once at pace, once with track changes on. Compare the three outputs against the source line by line. That exercise finds more problems than any typing test.

Then run five-minute rounds on dense text with punctuation, targeting 98% accuracy. If accuracy falls below that at 65 WPM, work at 58 until it holds - a legal document with two errors a page costs more to check than it saved to type.

## Frequently asked questions

### What typing speed do paralegals need?

Usually 50-65 WPM with high accuracy. Legal secretary roles sit slightly higher at 60-75 because transcription is a larger part of the job.

### Is 50 WPM enough for a paralegal job?

For many roles, yes, provided formatting and accuracy are clean. Firms that test usually care more about whether a citation and a numbered list come out identical every time.

### Do paralegal tests include legal terminology?

Sometimes. Work-sample tests use real clauses with defined terms and citations, which is why practising on legal text beats practising on random sentences.

### How do I improve accuracy on citations?

Type the same citation ten times from the source, checking character by character, rather than typing ten different citations once. Pattern repetition is what makes punctuation automatic.

### Does typing speed matter more than research skill?

No. Typing is a threshold you clear; the role is judged on document accuracy and research quality. That is why the bar sits at 50-65 rather than 75+.
