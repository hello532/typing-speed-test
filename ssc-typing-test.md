Several SSC recruitment exams include a typing test as a qualifying round, typically typing a passage in a set time to prove a minimum speed and accuracy. It is often held at a test centre and must be cleared to be considered for the post, no matter how well you did in the written exam.

SSC typing rounds commonly ask for around 35 words per minute in English, or roughly 30 words per minute in Hindi, with high accuracy. Some exams set the bar in a mix of speed and error count, so you must meet both to pass.

The passage is delivered on screen and the clock does not stop, so the goal is steady, error-free typing rather than bursts of speed. Practise with light punctuation and everyday vocabulary — the passages are ordinary prose rather than technical text.

Build your baseline with short daily sessions until you clear the required speed at 95%+ accuracy, then add a minute at a time to build stamina. Our government and bank typing pages cover the same clerical-test family; use them together for a rounded practice routine.

Exam-day skills transfer everywhere: how to pass a typing test and typing test tips apply the same accuracy-first strategy.

Post | Standard | Duration | Scoring
LDC / JSA | 35 WPM English or 30 WPM Hindi | 10 minutes | Words per minute with an error allowance
DEO / DEO Grade A | 8000 key depressions per hour | 15 minutes | Total key depressions against an 8000/hour rate

8000 key depressions per hour is about 26.7 WPM under the five-character convention, but it is measured as raw keystrokes including corrections you make, so a typist who backspaces constantly loses ground even at a good words-per-minute rate.

The typing test is qualifying: you either clear the standard or you do not, and the score does not add to your merit total. That changes the strategy completely. A candidate chasing 45 WPM with 12% errors fails; a candidate at 37 WPM with 3% errors passes.

Practise with the error budget in view: type at a pace where you can hold mistakes under the allowance, and never let a correction spree eat the clock. On the 10-minute LDC test, thirty seconds lost to backspacing is roughly 18 words you never typed.

## SSC Typing Test FAQ

### Is there a typing test in SSC exams?

Yes. Several SSC exams include a qualifying typing round where you must reach a minimum typing speed and accuracy, usually tested at a centre.

### What typing speed is needed for SSC?

Requirements vary by post and year, but roughly 35 words per minute in English or about 30 in Hindi is the commonly cited benchmark, with high accuracy.

### How can I practise for the SSC typing test?

Practise timed passages daily, keep accuracy above 95%, and build stamina for the full test length. Use a five-minute timer to mirror exam conditions.

### Is this SSC typing practice free?

Yes, free and no sign-up.

### What is the SSC CHSL typing speed?

For LDC and JSA posts, 35 words per minute in English or 30 in Hindi over a 10-minute test. For DEO posts the standard is 8000 key depressions per hour over 15 minutes.

### How many mistakes are allowed in the SSC typing test?

Roughly 5% of the words typed. Because the test is qualifying rather than merit-based, a slower run under the allowance passes and a fast run above it fails.
