What is a typing speed test?

A typing speed test measures how fast and how accurately you can type. The result is shown in words per minute (WPM), together with your typing accuracy and the number of errors you made. One "word" is counted as five characters, which is the standard used by most typing tests and employers.

How to improve your typing speed

Practice a few minutes every day, focus on accuracy first and speed will follow. Keep your fingers on the home row, look at the screen instead of the keyboard, and use all ten fingers. Most people type 35–45 WPM; reaching 60 WPM or more makes everyday writing and coding noticeably faster.

How is WPM calculated?

WPM = (correct characters / 5) / minutes used. Accuracy = correct characters / total characters typed. Only the text you type correctly counts toward your speed, so fix mistakes instead of racing past them.
