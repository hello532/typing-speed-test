## typing lessons

Good typing lessons build speed the safe way: one habit at a time. Touch typing means typing without looking at the keyboard, and it is the single biggest speed upgrade most people can make. Instead of hunting for keys with two fingers, you train all ten fingers so each key has a fixed finger and muscle memory takes over. The result is faster, steadier typing that also frees your eyes for the screen.

## Start every lesson with the home row

Every typing lesson plan starts in the same place: the home row. Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right fingers on J K L ; with your thumbs on the space bar. Feel for the small bumps on F and J; they tell your index fingers where to land without looking. Practice typing words made only of home row letters until you never have to think about it, because every other key is reached from this position.

## A free four week typing lesson plan

Week one: home row only, short one minute lessons, accuracy over speed. Week two: add the top row and the shift keys for capitals, two minute lessons. Week three: cover the bottom row and the number row, then try the number text set. Week four: mix sentences, quotes and punctuation, and test your WPM against week one. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is enough; most learners reach 40 to 60 WPM within a month of daily lessons.

## Typing lessons FAQ

### How long does it take to learn touch typing?

Most people reach a comfortable 40 to 60 WPM with 10 to 15 minutes of daily practice for three to four weeks. Reaching 80 WPM or more takes a few more months of regular use, but the daily time stays the same.

### Are these typing lessons free?

Yes. Every lesson is free, with no sign up, no account and no paywall. You can repeat each lesson as many times as you like, and your results are saved only in your own browser.

### How often should I take typing lessons?

Daily is best, but short. Ten to fifteen minutes every day beats a single long session once a week, because muscle memory is built by frequency rather than by total minutes.

### Do I need a special keyboard or a paid course?

No. Any standard keyboard works, and these free lessons cover the same finger placement as paid typing courses. Start with the home row (A S D F J K L ;) and add one row at a time.

### What is the difference between touch typing and hunt-and-peck?

Hunt-and-peck means looking at the keys and pressing them with one or two fingers, which is slow and tiring. Touch typing uses all ten fingers from the home row without looking, so typing becomes faster, more accurate and far less effort.
