## typing certificate

A typing certificate should not cost money or an email address. Here the whole chain is free and instant: take the one minute test, open the results panel, and press Print certificate. Your scores are computed in your own browser and the certificate is generated in the same browser, so nothing is uploaded and nobody has to verify your email. Print it for a class, save it as a PDF for a portfolio, or share it straight from the page. Employers and schools often call the same check a typing assessment, so a certificate from this page and an assessment result off the same run report the same WPM.

## What is on the certificate

Six measured fields: WPM (net words per minute, the standard 5-character-per-word measure), accuracy as a percentage, chars per minute, raw WPM before errors are subtracted, errors, and consistency, which is how stable your speed was through the test. The certificate also carries the test date and the test length, so anyone reading it knows exactly what was measured.

## How to use a typing certificate

Teachers can run a whole class through a one minute test and have every student print a certificate in the same lesson, with no accounts to create and no data leaving the room. Job applicants can attach one to a portfolio to back up a stated typing speed. Track yourself by printing one at the start of a practice month and another at the end; the two certificates on the same wall are the most honest progress report you can make.

## Typing certificate FAQ

### Is the typing certificate free?

Yes, completely free with no sign up, no account and no paywall. The test and the certificate are both generated in your browser.

### How do I get my typing certificate?

Finish the test, then click the Print certificate button in the results panel. Your browser's print dialog opens with the certificate ready; choose a printer or Save as PDF.

### What WPM do I need for a typing certificate?

There is no pass or fail; the certificate records whatever you scored. For office work, 40 to 60 WPM is typical; 70 WPM or above is fast. Accuracy matters more than raw speed, so aim for 95 percent or higher.

### Can I use it as proof for a job or school?

It is self-generated and not notarised, so treat it as evidence of a self-measured result rather than an official credential. Many employers accept it as a supporting document alongside a proctored test.

### Does it work for tests longer than one minute?

Yes. Take any duration from 15 seconds to 10 minutes and the certificate records the test length along with your scores, so longer tests print an equally valid certificate.

### Where is my typing data stored?

Nowhere outside your browser. Scores are computed locally and your past results are stored only in localStorage on this device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
