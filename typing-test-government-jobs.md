## Typing speed test for government jobs

Government typing exams test speed and accuracy on timed passages in English or Hindi. The most common bar is 30-35 WPM with a mistake cap, assessed over a five-minute passage. The SSC typing test and bank clerk typing test pages have role-specific details.

Converting to the standard metric: 30 WPM is 150 CPM or 9,000 KPH. The accuracy cap means one error in a hundred characters is the maximum acceptable rate.

## Exam format: what to expect on test day

Most government typing exams follow a fixed format: a passage of 2,000-4,000 characters typed over five to ten minutes. The scoring deducts one mark per mistake. Practising with the exact format removes the pacing anxiety on test day.

The data entry typing test and keystrokes per hour test provide the closest simulation for KPH-based roles.

## Two-week preparation plan

Week one: accuracy base. Daily rounds at 90% of comfortable speed with a hard 98% accuracy line. Practise in the exam language, with the same punctuation marks. Week two: pace and simulation. Lift speed 5% a day, hold the accuracy floor, run a full simulation daily.

Speed beyond the requirement does not earn extra marks in most government exams. Consistent accuracy over the full duration does. The typing speed requirements table marks these exams as accuracy-critical.
