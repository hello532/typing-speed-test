## Punctuation typing test

Punctuation marks sit on the hardest-to-reach keys and often need the Shift key, so they are the biggest source of typing errors. This punctuation typing test feeds you real sentences full of commas, full stops, apostrophes, quotes and brackets so you can practise them in context.

## Tips for accurate punctuation

Slow down slightly before a punctuation mark, keep your fingers anchored on the home row, and learn the exact reach for each symbol. Accuracy matters more than speed here, because a misplaced comma or bracket is costly to fix.

## Why punctuation is harder to type

Letters live on the home and middle rows, but commas, apostrophes, quotation marks and brackets force your fingers to the edges of the keyboard — often with the Shift key held down. That reach breaks your rhythm, which is exactly why dedicated punctuation practice pays off for anyone who writes emails, essays or code all day.

## Punctuation worth drilling

Focus on the marks you actually use: periods, commas, apostrophes in contractions, question marks, colons and parentheses. Practising sentences full of real punctuation trains the Shift-plus-symbol combinations until they feel automatic, so your writing flows without constant pauses to hunt for the right key.
