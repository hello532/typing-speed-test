# Punctuation Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/punctuation-typing-test.html

Drill commas, periods, quotes and symbols with a punctuation typing test. Punctuation is where accuracy drops most, so train it directly and watch your WPM improve.

Letters sit under your fingers; punctuation does not. The comma, full stop, apostrophe and semicolon need extended reaches and often a Shift press, and none of them appear often enough in normal practice to become automatic.

There is a second reason: punctuation carries meaning. A missing apostrophe changes a word, a wrong comma changes a clause, and a full stop in the wrong place ends a sentence early. Typists who treat it as decoration produce text that has to be re-read.

Mark | Where it is | Why it slows you down
Apostrophe ' | Right of the semicolon | Right little finger, already the weakest, with no Shift
Comma , | Right of M | Same finger as the apostrophe; frequent in real sentences
Full stop . | Right of the comma | Followed by a capital, so it is a two-key rhythm
Shifted symbols : ? ! " | Top row with Shift | Two keys at once, opposite hands, and a return to the home row

If your error report concentrates on the right-hand column of the keyboard, that is not a knowledge problem - it is a reach problem, and it responds to short daily drills.

Use real sentences, not symbol lists. A list of punctuation marks trains the key but not the rhythm; sentences train the transition from word to mark and back, which is what actually fails under time pressure.

Then run one round where you deliberately slow to 80% and check every mark. The next round, type normally and see whether the accuracy carried over. Two rounds is a session; ten minutes is enough.

Exam passages include capitals and punctuation, and an omitted character counts as an error on every mainstream test. Candidates who skip punctuation to gain speed usually lose more in the error allowance than they gain in WPM.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a punctuation typing test?

A test using sentences with full punctuation and capitals, so the score reflects how you handle marks rather than only letters.

### Why do I make so many punctuation errors?

Punctuation needs extended reaches, often with Shift, and appears too rarely in normal typing to become automatic. Right-hand marks are the usual weak spot.

### Is the punctuation test free?

Yes, no account, and results stay in your browser.

### Which punctuation marks slow typists down most?

The apostrophe and comma, both under the right little finger, and shifted symbols such as : ? ! which need two keys at once.

### Do punctuation errors count on exams?

Yes. An omitted or wrong mark is an error, and most tests apply an error allowance that a punctuation-skipping strategy will exhaust.
