## Why drill the top Hindi words

In almost every language, a few hundred words cover most of what people actually write. Hindi is no exception: these 200 words appear again and again in messages, documents, subtitles and web pages. If your fingers can produce them without thinking, your real-world Hindi typing speed rises immediately — long before you memorise any larger vocabulary.

Hindi high-frequency words are rich in matras and the conjunct consonants (क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ) that slow most typists down. Drilling these 200 words builds automaticity exactly where Hindi typing needs it.

## Top Hindi words FAQ

### Where does this Hindi word list come from?

From the open FrequencyWords dataset (MIT license), which counts word frequencies in OpenSubtitles transcripts. We filtered the raw ranking to native-script words and took the top 200.

### Are these really the most common Hindi words?

They are the most frequent words in subtitle transcripts — everyday spoken-style language. That makes them the most useful 200 words for typing practice, though formal written text has a slightly different profile.

### How should I use this list to get faster at Hindi typing?

Practise with the built-in test above: it serves these exact words in timed rounds. Short daily rounds with an accuracy-first approach build speed fastest.
