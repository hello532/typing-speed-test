# Contact

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/contact.html

One address, read by the two people who build and maintain this site. Use it for corrections, broken pages, citation questions and permission requests.

Write to hello@rerivo.com. It reaches the same inbox that receives the Search Console reports and the error alerts for this site, so a message about a broken page is read by whoever can fix it. We answer within two working days, usually the same day.

We do not run a contact form, a live chat or a support ticketing system. All three would require an account, a cookie and a database, and this site deliberately has none of them.

Wrong numbers. A benchmark that does not match its source paper, a conversion that does not multiply out, a percentile that contradicts the table next to it. Send the page and the figure; these get corrected and the correction is dated.
Broken pages. A test that will not start, a link that 404s, a layout that breaks on your device. Include the browser and the screen width if you can.
Accessibility. Anything you cannot operate with a keyboard, a screen reader or a large text size.
Attribution. If you are the author of a study we cite and you want the wording or the link changed, say which page and what it should say.

We cannot recover a typing history. Results live in your browser's localStorage and never reach a server, so when they are gone we have no copy to restore. Clearing site data, using a private window or switching devices starts a fresh history.

We cannot verify a certificate for a third party. The certificate this site prints is self-issued and says so on its face; it is not an accredited credential and there is no register to check it against.

We cannot remove a page from Google's index on your behalf. If you own a site we link to and want the link changed, write to us — we will update our page, and the change is picked up on the next crawl.

The typing test is free and has no account. Display advertising on some pages covers the hosting. Advertisers never see your typing results — there is nothing to see, because the test runs entirely in your browser. The privacy policy lists exactly which third parties are involved and how to opt out.

## Contact FAQ

### How fast do you reply?

Within two working days. Corrections to a stated figure are usually made the same day and the page's last-updated date changes with them.

### Can I reuse the benchmark table?

Yes, with attribution to the underlying studies. The table and the CSV are also published on GitHub so they can be cited without scraping the page.

### Do you accept guest posts or paid links?

No. We do not sell links and we do not publish contributed articles. Both would undercut the reason the benchmarks are worth citing.

### Where do I report an accessibility problem?

The same address. Say which page and which assistive technology you use; access issues are treated as bugs rather than feature requests.
