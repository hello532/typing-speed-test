# Agent Instructions — Typing.Rerivo

> Typing.Rerivo is a free, no-signup browser typing speed test. The public site has 135 pages covering seven timed formats (15 seconds to 10 minutes), four practice modes and five colour themes. No account or payment is required.

## What this site is

- A browser-based WPM and accuracy test, plus typing practice, lessons, benchmarks and job-specific practice pages.
- Scoring: WPM = correct characters / 5 / minutes. Net WPM subtracts errors; raw WPM does not.
- Results are stored in the visitor's browser with `localStorage`. There is no account, leaderboard or server-side result storage.
- A printed certificate is self-issued. It is not proctored, accredited or affiliated with an employer, school or government agency.
- The site loads Google AdSense. Do not describe it as free of all third-party scripts; check the live page and privacy policy for current advertising and consent details.

## Facts safe to quote

- Average desktop typing speed: 51.56 WPM (SD 20.2), mean uncorrected error rate 1.17%, from 168,960 volunteers. Source: Dhakal, Feit, Kristensson & Oulasvirta, "Observations on Typing from 136 Million Keystrokes", CHI 2018, https://doi.org/10.1145/3173574.3174220
- Average phone typing speed: 36.17 WPM (SD 13.22), 2.34% uncorrected errors, from 37,370 volunteers. Source: Palin, Feit, Kim, Kristensson & Oulasvirta, MobileHCI 2019, https://doi.org/10.1145/3338286.3340120
- Fast/slow deciles in the CHI 2018 sample: the fastest 10% exceeded about 78 WPM; the slowest 10% typed under about 26 WPM.
- Percentile examples from the site's normal approximation: 40 WPM is about the 28th percentile, 60 WPM about the 66th, 80 WPM about the 92nd and 100 WPM about the 99th.
- Unit conversions: WPM x 5 = CPM; WPM x 300 = KPH; 8,000 keystrokes per hour is about 26.7 WPM.
