## 1 minute typing test

The 1 minute typing test is the most popular way to check typing speed. Sixty seconds is long enough to measure your real speed and short enough to fit into any break. Type the passage as fast as you can without mistakes — the test counts your WPM, accuracy and errors automatically. When the minute is up, your result appears instantly and is saved in this browser so you can compare it with your next attempt.

## Why 60 seconds?

A one minute test balances two things: it is long enough that a single lucky or unlucky sentence does not dominate the result, and short enough that you stay focused the whole time. Shorter tests such as 15 or 30 seconds are fun for quick bursts, but a 60 second test is what most people mean when they say typing speed. Recruiters and typing certificates almost always use a one minute test.

## What your 1 minute score means

Under 30 WPM marks a beginner still building touch typing foundations; 30 to 40 is typical for casual typists, and 40 to 60 covers the average adult range where most office work feels comfortable. Above 60 you are faster than roughly three quarters of test takers, 80-plus is professional territory for transcription and data entry, and anything past 100 approaches competitive speeds. Whatever your number, compare it against the benchmarks in our average typing speed guide rather than judging by a single lucky round.

## Typing test FAQ

### What is a 1 minute typing test?

A 60-second timed round: type the passage shown for exactly one minute, then get your WPM, accuracy and error breakdown instantly. One minute is the most-quoted test length, so scores compare directly across sites and job requirements.

### How is WPM scored on the 1 minute test?

Correct characters divided by 5, divided by the one elapsed minute. Because the test lasts exactly sixty seconds the maths is simple: 300 correct characters equals 60 WPM. The results panel shows both raw WPM and net WPM with errors subtracted.

### Is 1 minute the right test length?

For most people, yes: 60 seconds balances speed bursts against fatigue and matches the length employers and benchmarks use. Take longer 3 or 5 minute tests when you want a steadier, hiring-grade number.

### Is the 1 minute test free?

Yes — unlimited 1 minute rounds, no sign up, no ads in the typing area. Your history stays in your own browser.

### How can I raise my 1 minute score?

Warm up with one throwaway round, then focus on accuracy: at 95%+ accuracy speed follows. Keep your fingers on the home row, look at the text not your hands, and practise daily with typing drills.

### Is a 1 minute typing test accurate?

Yes, for a quick check. Sixty seconds is long enough that one lucky sentence does not decide the result, and it is the length employers and typing certificates use, so your score compares directly. Take a 3 or 5 minute test when you want a steadier number.
