Test your typing speed in just 30 seconds. A fast, no-frills WPM check when you want a quick snapshot of your typing speed without committing to a full minute.

## 30 second typing test

The 30 second typing test is the quickest way to get a WPM estimate. Thirty seconds is enough to type roughly 20-45 words, which gives a usable sample when you are short on time. For a steadier score that averages out fast bursts, try the 1 minute typing test or the 5 minute typing test.

## How accurate is a 30 second test?

A 30 second test is a good snapshot but not the most stable measure - one fast or slow sentence sways the result more than in a longer test. Use it for quick daily checks, and pair it with longer rounds from typing practice to track real progress.

## Tips for a faster 30 second sprint

Warm up first, keep your eyes on the text rather than your hands, and prioritise accuracy over raw speed since errors cost time to fix. To build real speed, our typing exercises progressively train common words and patterns.

## When to choose the 30 second test

The 30 second round shines in three situations: a rapid self-check before a job application asks for your WPM, a warm-up lap before a 5 minute typing test, and a low-pressure daily streak you can keep even on busy days. Because one round takes less than a minute including the result screen, it is also the easiest length to repeat — take three back-to-back sprints and average them for a steadier number than any single attempt. Pair it with structured typing practice when one weak key keeps dragging your score down.

## 30 second typing test FAQ

### Is a 30 second typing test accurate?

It gives a reasonable snapshot of your speed, but a short test is more easily affected by a single fast or slow sentence. For a more stable WPM, use a 1, 2 or 5 minute test.

### How many words do you type in 30 seconds?

Most people type 20-45 words in 30 seconds depending on their speed, which is enough for a quick WPM estimate.
