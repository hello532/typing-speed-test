# Privacy Policy

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/privacy-policy.html

This page explains what Typing.Rerivo does and does not collect. The short version: nothing about you is collected at all.

There is no account system, no sign up, no email collection and no contact form on the typing test itself. We do not run our own analytics, we do not fingerprint devices, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Some pages carry display advertising served by Google AdSense. That is the only third-party code on the site, and it works through Google's own cookies rather than anything we set. The next section says exactly what that means and how to turn it off.

When you take a test, your text input, your typing speed and your accuracy are computed entirely inside your own browser by JavaScript running on this page. The results are never transmitted to any server.

Your typing history, your chosen settings and your high score are saved in your browser's localStorage, which lives on your device only. Clearing your browser data or using a private window removes all of it. Nothing of yours reaches a database, because there is no database.

If you use the print certificate feature, the certificate is generated in your browser and sent straight to your printer or to your browser's save as PDF dialog; it is not uploaded anywhere.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any advertising cookie is set, and can change their choice at any time from the link on that message. Where consent is refused, ads are served in a non-personalised form or not at all.

Advertising never appears inside the typing area, and no advertising code receives your typing results — they exist only in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may record standard request logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) as part of serving the pages, as any web host does. Those logs are governed by GitHub's own privacy policy and are not shared with or used by us.

We submit page URLs to IndexNow so search engines can discover the site faster. That submission contains only public URLs, no personal information.

## Privacy FAQ

### Do you use cookies?

No. Settings are stored in browser localStorage, which is not a cookie and is not sent to any server with your requests.

### Do you run Google Analytics or any tracking?

No analytics, no advertising scripts and no tracking pixels run on this site.

### Is the typing test suitable for children?

Yes. There are no accounts, no data collection and no advertising, and the practice texts are ordinary sentences and quotations.

### How do I delete my typing history?

Clear your browser's site data for typing.rerivo.com, or use a private or incognito window, and nothing is retained.

### How can I contact you?

By email at hello@rerivo.com for any privacy question or removal request.
