## Keyboard typing test

A keyboard typing test checks how fast you type on your keyboard and how accurate you are. It works the same on laptop, mechanical and full-size keyboards. Measuring regularly shows whether a new keyboard or layout actually improves your speed.

## Test any keyboard, not just the one you own

A keyboard test answers one question honestly: how fast are your hands on this keyboard? Laptop keyboards, mechanical switches and full-size boards all have different travel and feedback, and your WPM shifts with them. Test the keyboard you use for work, then test the one you game on; the difference tells you which one your hands actually like.

## Keyboard typing tips

Keep your wrists relaxed and fingers anchored on the home row. Use all ten fingers rather than hunting and pecking. A comfortable keyboard with good key travel helps, but consistent practice matters far more than the hardware.

## Works with any keyboard layout

This keyboard typing test runs on QWERTY, AZERTY, Dvorak and Colemak alike — it simply measures the characters you type against the target text. Membrane, mechanical or laptop scissor-switch, the physical board doesn't change the score. What matters is muscle memory: knowing where each key sits without looking so your hands stay anchored on the home row.

## Build true touch typing

Touch typing means typing without watching your fingers. Use the small bumps on the F and J keys to find the home row by feel, then return to it after every reach. The on-screen keyboard below highlights the next key so you can gradually stop looking down at your real keyboard and trust your hands instead.

## Typing test FAQ

### Does the keyboard type affect my score?

Technique matters more than hardware, but a comfortable keyboard with good key travel can help you type faster and more accurately.

### Is this keyboard typing test free?

Yes, free with no sign up. Results stay in your own browser only.

### Can I test a mechanical keyboard?

Yes — this test works on any keyboard, so it's great for comparing a new mechanical board against your old one.

### Laptop or mechanical keyboard — which scores higher?

Mechanical keyboards usually add 3 to 8 WPM from cleaner key feel and fewer missed presses, but the gap shrinks with practice. Test both here and keep the one whose accuracy stays higher after ten rounds, not just the faster-feeling one.
