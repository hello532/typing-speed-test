## How to calculate typing speed (WPM)

Typing speed in words per minute (WPM) is worked out with one simple formula: WPM = (characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Every five characters count as one &lsquo;word&rsquo;, so longer words are worth more. Net WPM subtracts your errors first: net WPM = ((correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes). Employers use net WPM, because a mistake you have to fix costs time.

## WPM, CPM and KPH conversions

The three common typing-speed units are all fixed multiples of each other, so you can convert any of them by hand:

From | To | Formula
CPM | WPM | WPM = CPM ÷ 5
WPM | CPM | CPM = WPM × 5
CPM | KPH | KPH = CPM × 60
WPM | KPH | KPH = WPM × 300
WPM | | WPM = (characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes

So 60 WPM = 300 CPM = 18,000 KPH. These are the standard conversions used by typing certificates and data entry employers.

## Worked examples

If you type 300 characters in 1 minute, that is 300 ÷ 5 = 60 WPM (or 18,000 KPH). If you type 1,200 characters in 3 minutes, that is (1,200 ÷ 5) ÷ 3 = 80 WPM. If you make 12 mistakes over that run, your net speed is (1,200 &minus; 12) ÷ 5 ÷ 3 = 79 WPM.

## Typing speed calculator FAQ

### How do I calculate my typing speed in WPM?

WPM = (characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed. Every 5 characters count as one word, so 300 characters in 1 minute is 60 WPM.

### How do I convert CPM to WPM?

Divide characters per minute by 5. So 250 CPM is 50 WPM, and 400 CPM is 80 WPM.

### How do I convert WPM to KPH?

Multiply WPM by 300. For example, 60 WPM equals 18,000 keystrokes per hour.

### What is the difference between gross and net WPM?

Gross WPM counts every character you typed; net WPM subtracts your errors first. Employers almost always use net WPM because it reflects usable output.
