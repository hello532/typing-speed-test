# Easy Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/easy-typing-test.html

A simple, easy typing test for beginners. Start with short rounds and common words, see your WPM and accuracy instantly, and build confidence at your own pace.

## Easy typing test for beginners

If you are just starting out, long passages and hard words can be discouraging. This easy typing test keeps things simple with short 15 or 30 second rounds and common everyday words, so you can get a quick WPM score and feel progress fast.

## Build speed step by step

Begin with the shortest rounds and the words mode, focus on accuracy, and only then stretch to a 1 minute typing test. A few relaxed minutes each day beat one long frustrating session, and your saved history shows every improvement.

## A gentle starting point for beginners

If longer tests feel intimidating, start here. Short durations and simple, everyday sentences let you build rhythm without pressure. There's no account, no difficulty spike and no penalty for a slow start — just type at a pace that feels comfortable and watch your numbers climb a little each time.

## Building typing confidence

Confidence comes from repetition. Begin with the 15- or 30-second timer, keep your eyes on the screen, and let the on-screen keyboard guide your next key. As clean runs become routine, stretch to the one-minute test. Small wins stack up fast, and before long the keys you once hunted for will feel automatic.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes this an easy typing test?

It offers short rounds and a simple common-words mode, so beginners get a quick, encouraging WPM result without long or difficult passages.

### I am a slow typist — where do I start?

Start with a 15 or 30 second round in the words mode, aim for accuracy first, and build up to longer tests as you improve.

### Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no sign up. Your results stay in your own browser.

### How easy is easy — what WPM should I expect first?

Most beginners score 15 to 25 WPM on their first try with simple words, and that is normal. After a week of short daily rounds most reach 30+, the point where typing starts feeling useful instead of painful.
