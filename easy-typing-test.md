# Easy Typing Test

Source: https://typing.rerivo.com/easy-typing-test.html

A simple, easy typing test for beginners. Start with short rounds and common words, see your WPM and accuracy instantly, and build confidence at your own pace.

Three adjustments: common short words, no digits or unusual punctuation, and a one-minute default so the round ends before fatigue arrives. The point is to remove everything except the skill being built - finding keys without looking.

Easy is not the same as slow. Type at the pace where you make almost no errors; if accuracy is below 95%, slow down further rather than pushing. At this stage speed is a side effect of accuracy, not a separate goal.

Week | Daily drill | What should change
1 | Two 60-second easy rounds, hands covered for one | You stop looking at the keyboard for most words
2 | Same, plus one home-row-only round | Accuracy above 95% consistently
3 | Two-minute rounds, no backspace allowed | Corrections stop eating your time
4 | Three-minute rounds, then one five-minute check | A reproducible number you can quote

Looking at the keyboard. Every glance costs a beat and prevents the mental map from forming. Cover your hands once a day, even if it feels slow.
Backspacing through every slip. At beginner speeds, corrections cost more than the errors. Practise finishing rounds without correcting.
Two-finger typing. It works to about 35-40 WPM and then stops. If you are under 30 WPM, learning the home row now is cheaper than fixing it later.

Once you hold 95% accuracy at 30 WPM on easy text, move to the beginner test with normal sentences, then to the one-minute test. The easy page is a starting gate, not a home.

If you are already above 40 WPM, this page will flatter you - switch to the hard typing test, which includes capitals, digits and long words.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes this an easy typing test?

Common short words, no digits or unusual punctuation, and a one-minute default. It isolates key-finding from everything else.

### I am a slow typist - where do I start?

Two 60-second rounds a day, hands covered for one of them, and no backspacing. That combination removes the two habits that cap beginners at 30-40 WPM.

### Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no ads in the typing area. Results stay in your browser.

### What WPM should I expect first?

Most untrained adults start at 20-30 WPM with 90-95% accuracy. Below 20 WPM is usually two-finger typing rather than a lack of ability.

### When should I move to a harder test?

When you hold 95% accuracy at 30 WPM on easy text. Moving earlier just moves the errors to harder text.
