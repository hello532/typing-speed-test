## Online typing test

An online typing test runs entirely in your browser — there's nothing to download or install. Pick a duration and text type, start typing, and get your WPM and accuracy instantly. It works on any device with a keyboard.

## Benefits of an online test

Because it's browser-based, you can test your typing speed anywhere — at work, school or home — without software. Your results are saved locally so you can track progress over time, completely private.

## How to improve your online typing speed

The fastest way to raise your WPM online is short, regular sessions. Five focused minutes a day beats one long weekly grind. Keep your fingers on the home row (ASDF JKL;), glance at the screen instead of the keys, and let your weakest fingers — the pinkies and ring fingers — do their share of the work. Speed follows accuracy, so slow down whenever your error count climbs.

## Who uses an online typing test

Students check their words-per-minute before online exams, remote workers warm up before a day of emails, call-centre and data-entry applicants rehearse for employer screening tests, and programmers measure how quickly they move through code. Because the test is browser-based and private, anyone can benchmark their speed in under a minute and come back to track steady improvement.

## Online typing test vs installed typing software

An online typing test needs nothing installed, which matters when you are checking speed on a work laptop, a borrowed machine or a Chromebook.

No install or admin rights - the test runs in the browser.
Same engine everywhere - your score does not depend on which machine you used.
Nothing leaves the device - results are stored in your own browser, not uploaded.

The one trade-off is the keyboard itself. Typing on an unfamiliar keyboard can cost 5-10 WPM, so if you are comparing scores, compare them on the same machine.

## Typing test FAQ

### Do I need to install anything?

No. This online typing test runs fully in your browser on any device with a keyboard.

### Is the online typing test free?

Yes, completely free with no sign up. Results are saved only in your own browser.

### Does it work on mobile?

It works best with a physical keyboard, but you can try it on a tablet with an attached keyboard too.

### Does the online test work offline after loading?

Yes — once the page loads, the test runs fully in your browser with no further network use. Your results save locally too, so a dropped connection mid-round never loses your score.
