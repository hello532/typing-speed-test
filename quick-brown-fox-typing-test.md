## What is the quick brown fox sentence?

&lsquo;The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog&rsquo; is a pangram: a sentence that uses all 26 letters of the alphabet at least once. Because it covers the whole keyboard it has been the standard typing drill for over a century, and it is still used to test typewriters, keyboards and fonts. Practising it warms up every finger before a longer typing test.

Type it a few times here - the test scrolls through alphabet-rich sentences in the same spirit, so your warm-up trains every letter rather than only the common ones.

## Why practise with pangrams?

Ordinary sentences repeat the same handful of common letters and rarely touch q, z, x or j. A pangram forces every key into the run, which is exactly what you need before a speed attempt: it surfaces the awkward keys while the stakes are low. That is why &lsquo;quick brown fox&rsquo; games appear in almost every typing tutor and school typing class.

For a targeted version, the keyboard typing test highlights the next key and the correct finger, and the easy typing test uses simple high-frequency words if the pangram still feels hard.

## Quick brown fox and keyboard checking

Because the pangram contains every letter, typing it is the fastest way to confirm that no key is broken or stuck: if a letter is missing from the output, that key is faulty. Try it with capitals and punctuation too - &lsquo;The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.&rsquo; - to exercise the shift keys and the full stop. Then move on to a full typing speed test to get your WPM.

## Quick brown fox FAQ

### What is the quick brown fox sentence?

&lsquo;The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog&rsquo; is a pangram &mdash; a sentence that contains every letter of the alphabet. It has been the classic typing-practice sentence for over a century.

### Why is the quick brown fox used for typing?

Because it uses all 26 letters, it exercises every key and finger in one short line, making it an ideal warm-up and a fast way to check that your keyboard works.

### Is the quick brown fox test good for beginners?

It is a good warm-up, but the unusual letters (q, z, x) make it harder than common-word practice. Beginners may prefer the easy typing test first.

### How many words are in the quick brown fox sentence?

The classic sentence is nine words: The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. It contains 35 letters.

### Can I use it to test a keyboard?

Yes. If a letter is missing from what you type, that key is faulty. Include the full stop and capitals to test shift and punctuation keys as well.
