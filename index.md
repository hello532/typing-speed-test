## How to improve your typing speed

Take a short test every day. Start with a 1 minute typing test, then try 2 or 5 minutes as you get faster. Focus on accuracy first — speed follows accuracy. Keep your fingers on the home row and look at the screen, not the keyboard. Switch to quotes or numbers mode to practice punctuation and digits, which are the most common slowdowns.

## What is a good typing speed?

An average typing speed is about 40 WPM. Typing at 60 WPM is faster than most people and good for most office work. Above 80 WPM is fast, and professional typists and developers often reach 90–120 WPM. Children usually start around 20–30 WPM and improve quickly with a few minutes of daily practice.

## Tips to type faster and more accurately

Fast typing is built on good habits. Keep your fingers on the home row, use all ten fingers, and glance at the screen rather than the keyboard. Take a typing test every day for ten to fifteen minutes and always value accuracy over raw speed, because fixing mistakes costs more time than typing a little slower. ## Typing test FAQ

### What is a typing test?

A typing test measures how fast and accurately you type. You type a passage of text while the test counts your words per minute (WPM), accuracy and errors.

### How does this site calculate WPM?

The standard way: (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Five characters count as one word, so longer words move the score more. Net WPM subtracts errors while raw WPM ignores them — both are shown after every round.

### How long should a typing test be?

1 minute is the most popular length and is enough for a quick check. Longer tests (3, 5 or 10 minutes) give a more stable result because they average out short bursts of speed and fatigue.

### How do I get faster at typing?

Daily 10 to 15 minute sessions, accuracy before speed, relaxed wrists, fingers anchored on ASDF JKL;, all ten fingers instead of hunting and pecking. Most people gain 10+ WPM in a month.
