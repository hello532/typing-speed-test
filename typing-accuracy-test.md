## Typing accuracy test

A typing accuracy test shows what percentage of characters you type correctly, alongside your WPM and error count. Accuracy is often more valuable than raw speed — a fast typist who makes many mistakes is slower overall once corrections are counted.

## How to improve accuracy

Slow down slightly and aim for zero errors first; speed follows naturally once your fingers learn the correct movements. Focus on the keys you miss most, and keep your eyes on the screen so you can catch mistakes as they happen.

## Why accuracy beats raw speed

A blazing WPM means little if half your words need fixing. Every mistake costs a backspace and a retype, so real-world typing speed depends far more on accuracy than on peak pace. This test shows accuracy next to WPM and counts your errors, so you can see exactly where clean typing is quietly slowing you down.

## How to type more accurately

Start slower than feels natural and let correct keystrokes build muscle memory — rushing only teaches your fingers the wrong moves. Watch for your personal problem keys (often the top row and punctuation), keep a light, even rhythm, and resist the urge to glance at the keyboard. Aim to hold accuracy above 97% before you push for speed.

## Typing test FAQ

### What is a good typing accuracy?

Aim for 95% or higher. Professional typists usually stay above 97% accuracy while maintaining good speed.

### Why does accuracy matter more than speed?

Every error needs correcting, which costs time. High accuracy means your effective speed is close to your raw speed.

### Is this accuracy test free?

Yes, free with no sign up. Results are stored only in your browser.

### What accuracy should I aim for before pushing speed?

Hold 97% or higher first; only then add pace. Below 95%, every extra WPM costs more correction time than it saves, so the score looks faster while real output slows down.
