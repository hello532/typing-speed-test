## What typing speed do government jobs require?

Government typing requirements vary by post and country, but the common shape is: a minimum of 30-40 WPM for entry-level administrative roles, and 40-60 WPM with high accuracy for clerical, secretarial and data entry positions. Where the job is mainly data entry, the test may be a numeric one scored in keystrokes per hour instead.

Post type | Typical requirement
Entry / general clerical | 30-40 WPM
Clerical / admin | 40-50 WPM
Secretarial / stenography | 50-70 WPM
Data entry | 8,000-10,000 KPH

Speed is only half of it: government tests usually require 95-98% accuracy, and some subtract errors, so a clean run beats a fast messy one. Requirements differ by department and country - always check the official notification for your exam.

## How government typing tests are scored

Government typing tests are timed, commonly 3 to 5 minutes, and scored on net speed - the words per minute remaining after errors are deducted. Many use a fixed passage, and some allow no backspace, so typing it correctly the first time is essential. Because the standard is net speed, accuracy training is the fastest route to passing, not raw bursts of speed.

Practise at the exam length. Warm up with a 1 minute test, then run 3 and 5 minutes to build the stamina a scored attempt needs.

## How to prepare for a government typing test

Work on accuracy until you can complete a passage cleanly, then raise speed. Train the home row and touch typing so you are not watching your hands, and if the post is data entry, drill the keypad with the 10 key typing test. Test yourself under the same conditions as the exam - same length, no restarts - and check your standing against the average typing speed. Your practice history stays in your browser.

## Government typing test FAQ

### What typing speed do government jobs require?

Entry-level government and clerical roles commonly require 30 to 40 WPM, while clerical, secretarial and data roles ask for 40 to 70 WPM with high accuracy. Check the official notification for your specific exam.

### How long is a government typing test?

Government typing tests are typically 3 to 5 minutes long and are scored on net words per minute after errors are deducted.

### Is accuracy important in government typing tests?

Yes. Most government tests require 95% to 98% accuracy, and many subtract errors from your score, so accuracy matters as much as speed.

### Do government jobs use a numeric typing test?

Data entry posts often use a numeric keypad (10 key) test scored in keystrokes per hour, while general clerical posts use a text passage measured in words per minute.

### How do I pass a government typing test?

Practise at the exam length, prioritise accuracy over raw speed, learn to touch type so you do not look at the keyboard, and train the number pad if the post is data entry.
