## Free typing test, no sign up

Many typing sites hide results behind an account or bury the test under ads. This free typing test does neither: you land on the page, start typing, and get your WPM and accuracy the moment the timer ends. Nothing is locked and nothing is uploaded to a server.

## Practise as much as you want

Because the test is free forever, you can run it every day to build speed and accuracy. Pick any length from 15 seconds to 10 minutes and switch between sentences, quotes, numbers and words to train your weak spots.

## Completely free, no sign-up required

There's no account, no email, no paywall and no trial that quietly expires. Open the page and start typing — every duration, text mode, language and theme is available immediately and forever. Your results stay on your own device, so practising costs you nothing and shares nothing.

## Everything you get for free

Free here means the full tool: timed tests from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, real sentences, quotes, numbers and word drills, live WPM and accuracy, an on-screen keyboard, a results graph and locally saved history. It's the complete typing test experience without a single hidden cost.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this typing test really free?

Yes. There is no sign up, no account, no paywall and no ads inside the typing area. You can use it as often as you like.

### Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed. Your past results are saved only in your own browser using localStorage.

### What can I measure?

You get words per minute (WPM), accuracy, characters per minute and error counts after every attempt.

### Is there a catch — why is it free?

No catch: the test runs entirely in your browser with no accounts, no servers and no ads in the typing area, so there is almost nothing to pay for. Optional display ads outside the test area keep the site running without ever locking features.
