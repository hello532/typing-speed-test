## Turkish typing test

A Turkish typing test measures how fast and accurately you type real Turkish sentences — with the special letters ç, ğ, ı, ö, ş and ü, and the famous dotted İ and dotless ı that distinguish Turkish from every other Latin-script language. Your result shows in words per minute, where every 5 characters counts as one word.

## Turkish Q, F and standard keyboards

Turkey has two native layouts: the Turkish Q keyboard, which places ç, ğ, ı, ö, ş and ü on dedicated keys to the right of the main block, and the Turkish F keyboard, designed for maximum Turkish letter efficiency and still required in some official exams. This test measures the text you produce, so it works with Turkish Q, Turkish F, or any standard QWERTY with a Turkish input layout.

## The dotted İ and dotless ı

Turkish is the only language that distinguishes a dotted capital İ from a dotless lowercase ı as separate letters. Mixing them up changes word meaning, and they slow down typists trained on other layouts. Sentence practice with real Turkish text builds the automatic habit of hitting the right one every time.

## How to improve Turkish typing speed

Practise with real sentences so the special letters stop being special: ç, ğ, ş and the İ/ı pair should feel as automatic as a and e. Keep your eyes on the screen, accuracy first, and use daily timed rounds — Turkish suffix chains (-lar/-ler, -miş, -arak) repeat constantly and quickly become single motions.
