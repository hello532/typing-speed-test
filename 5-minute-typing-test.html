5 minute typing test

The 5 minute typing test is the best way to measure your real, sustained typing speed. Over five minutes your score settles down: a single fast or slow sentence no longer dominates the result, and natural fatigue is factored in. This is why many employers and typing certifications prefer a 3-5 minute test over a 60 second one.

Who should use a 5 minute test?

If you type for a living — data entry, transcription, programming, customer support — the 5 minute test reflects your working speed far better than a short burst. Take it a few times a week and watch your WPM and accuracy trend in the saved history below.

Build typing endurance

A five-minute test measures something a quick sprint can't: how well you hold speed and accuracy as your hands tire. Pace yourself, keep your posture relaxed and your wrists level, and breathe. Finishing strong on the five-minute test is a reliable sign your typing is ready for long real-world sessions of writing, coding or data entry.

Typing test FAQ

Is a 5 minute typing test more accurate?

Yes. Longer tests average out short bursts of speed and moments of fatigue, so a 5 minute result is more stable and representative of your true typing speed than a 15 or 60 second test.

What is a good 5 minute WPM?

40 WPM is average, 60+ WPM is good, and 80+ WPM is professional level. Maintaining high accuracy over 5 minutes matters more than peak speed.

Is this typing test free?

Yes, completely free with no sign up and no ads inside the typing area. Your results are stored only in your own browser.

Does the test save my progress?

Your last results are saved locally in this browser (localStorage) so you can track improvement over time. Nothing is sent to a server.
