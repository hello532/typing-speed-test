## Why drill the top Spanish words

In almost every language, a few hundred words cover most of what people actually write. Spanish is no exception: these 200 words appear again and again in messages, documents, subtitles and web pages. If your fingers can produce them without thinking, your real-world Spanish typing speed rises immediately — long before you memorise any larger vocabulary.

Spanish high-frequency words include the accented vowels (qué, más, está) and the ñ that make Spanish typing distinct. Getting fast at these 200 words means your fingers stop hesitating at the accent keys.

## Top Spanish words FAQ

### Where does this Spanish word list come from?

From the open FrequencyWords dataset (MIT license), which counts word frequencies in OpenSubtitles transcripts. We filtered the raw ranking to native-script words and took the top 200.

### Are these really the most common Spanish words?

They are the most frequent words in subtitle transcripts — everyday spoken-style language. That makes them the most useful 200 words for typing practice, though formal written text has a slightly different profile.

### How should I use this list to get faster at Spanish typing?

Practise with the built-in test above: it serves these exact words in timed rounds. Short daily rounds with an accuracy-first approach build speed fastest.
